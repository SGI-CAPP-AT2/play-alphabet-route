How to play

  1. Choose a letter tile to start your route.
  2. Slide across neighboring tiles, or tap them one at a time.
  3. Visit every letter in alphabetical order. The order is checked when you finish, so start with A.
  4. Coins add 1 point, stars add 2 points, and rings subtract 1 point. Undo or reset your route anytime.
